Admin Settings

Chat history and settings are stored only in this browser on this device. Up to 16 recent messages (at most 8000 characters), your instructions, examples and any attached image are sent to your configured Worker when you chat. Saved facts stay on this device unless you enable Use saved facts in AI replies; then up to 2000 characters are sent with each message. Photos are not saved in chat history. This Admin panel is local settings, not a password-protected login. Do not paste an API key here. Protect the Worker endpoint before sharing this app publicly.

Off by default. When on, up to 2000 characters of saved facts are sent to your configured AI endpoint with each message. Facts are edited manually; they are not learned automatically or synced across devices. Turning this off stops future sharing. Deleting chat history does not delete saved facts; clear this field to remove them.

Image understanding needs the connected vision-capable Gemini model. Voice input depends on Chrome speech recognition and may not work on every mobile browser. Voice output uses your phone's installed voices.
